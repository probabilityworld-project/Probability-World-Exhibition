📚 Mathematical Concepts

1. Probability

Probability is the measure of how likely an event is to happen.

P(E) = Favourable Outcomes / Total Possible Outcomes

2. Theoretical Probability

It is the probability calculated mathematically before performing an experiment.

3. Experimental Probability

It is calculated using the actual results obtained after performing an experiment.

4. Sample Space

The set of all possible outcomes of an experiment is called its sample space.

5. Event

An event is a particular outcome or a collection of outcomes of an experiment.

⚖️ Theoretical vs Experimental Probability

Explore the difference between mathematical predictions and actual experimental results.

📘 Theoretical Probability

Calculated before performing an experiment.

P(E) = Favourable Outcomes / Total Outcomes

🔬 Experimental Probability

Calculated using actual experimental results.

P(E) = Observed Outcomes / Total Trials

📊 Key Differences

Basis Theoretical Experimental
Based On Mathematical calculation Actual observations
Calculation Expected outcomes Observed outcomes
Trials Not required Required
Result Expected probability Actual probability

🎲 Practical Dice Example

Suppose a fair dice is rolled 60 times and the number 6 appears 8 times.

Theoretical Probability:

1 / 6 = 16.67%

Experimental Probability:

8 / 60 = 13.33%

More trials can help experimental probability approach theoretical probability.
