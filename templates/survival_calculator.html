How is it calculated?

P(survive to y | alive at x) = (ly ÷ lx) × 100

lx = number of life-table survivors at current age x. ly = number of life-table survivors at target age y. The ratio gives a conditional survival probability for the selected life table.

Important: What this result means

This is a population-level statistical estimate based on the mortality assumptions in the selected life table. It is not a personal prediction, medical assessment, or guarantee of how long any individual will live. A person's actual outcome can differ because of health, living conditions, behaviour, environment, and many other factors.
